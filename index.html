03 · Selected Problems

From the lab: an automated worm-tracking pipeline.

Quantifying C. elegans chemotaxis at scale: turning raw video into labelled behavior, automatically, across thousands of frames.

I combined DeepLabCut keypoint tracking with SAM2 (run locally on its weights, no API), feeding the tracked body points as visual point-prompts to the segmentation model — so each frame is segmented without any clicks or text input.

Skeletonization on the resulting masks yields a posture per frame; propagation of posture along the body gives forward/reverse classification, which feeds into chemotaxis-level behaviors scored against a known concentration gradient.
